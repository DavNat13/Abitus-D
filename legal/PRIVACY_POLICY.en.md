# Abitus-D privacy policy

Version 2026-11. Date: November 22, 2026.

Draft for personal use. If the app is shared beyond the author's circle of friends, a professional must review this text.

## 1. Controller and contact

Abitus-D is a personal and free app with no commercial purpose. Contact for any question or complaint: app.abitus.d@gmail.com.

## 2. What data we process and why

- Email and password (stored only as an Argon2id hash): to create your account and let you sign in.
- Language, units and preferences: to adapt the app to you.
- Workouts, sets, loads and routines: so the app works and computes your progress.
- Devices with an active session (name, app version, dates): to protect your account and warn you about new sign-ins.
- IP address, stored only as a one-way keyed hash: to limit abuse.

Later the app may process simplified GPS routes, heart rate and step summaries, and body measurements. That data will only be used if you give a separate, revocable consent when you turn it on.

## 3. Legal basis and consent

When you register you accept this policy and the terms of use, and you confirm that you are 18 or older. We store the accepted version and the date.

## 4. Who we share it with

No one, except the technical providers that run the service:

- Vercel: hosts the server.
- Neon: hosts the database (Sao Paulo region, Brazil).
- Google (Gmail): sends the account emails.

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics and no sale of data.

## 5. Where and for how long

Your data is stored on your phone and in the service database while you have an account. Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and technical security records are deleted periodically. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from the server. Backups are purged according to their retention cycle.

## 6. Your rights

You can access, correct and export your data, withdraw consents and delete your account. Some of these actions will be available in the app; until then, you can request them by email.

## 7. Security

We use encrypted connections, Argon2id passwords, per-device sessions you can end and rotating tokens. No measure is infallible, but we protect your data as well as we can.

## 8. Minimum age

The app is only for people aged 18 or older.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will tell you in the app and by email, and ask you to accept it again.
